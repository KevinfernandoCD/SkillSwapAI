## Introduction

**SkillSwap** is a peer-to-peer skill exchange platform that connects learners and teachers through structured learning sessions. Whether you want to teach what you know or learn from others, SkillSwap provides the tools and community to make skill sharing effective and engaging.

### Key Features
- **Skill Management**: Add, edit, and organize your teaching and learning skills
- **Session Creation**: Host or join learning sessions with other users
- **Exchange System**: Propose skill swaps with other community members
- **Real-time Messaging**: Communicate with other users through integrated chat
- **AI-Powered Matching**: Intelligent recommendations for connections and sessions
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your learning journey and achievements

## Getting Started

### 1. Account Creation

1. **Visit the Homepage**: Navigate to the SkillSwap website
2. **Click "Get Started"**: This will take you to the signup page
3. **Fill in Your Details**:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Profile Picture (optional)
4. **Complete Registration**: Click "Create Account" to finish

### 2. First Login

1. **Enter Credentials**: Use your email and password to log in
2. **Welcome Dashboard**: You'll be redirected to your personalized dashboard
3. **Profile Setup**: Complete your profile by adding:
   - Bio/About section
   - Location
   - Profile picture

### 3. Initial Setup

After your first login, you should:

1. **Add Your Skills**: Go to "My Skills" section
2. **Set Learning Goals**: Define what you want to learn
3. **Explore the Community**: Browse other users and sessions
4. **Complete Your Profile**: Add additional information to help others find you

## Skills Management

The "My Skills" section is where you define what you can teach and what you want to learn.

### Adding Teaching Skills

1. **Navigate to My Skills**: Click "My Skills" in the sidebar
2. **Select Teaching Tab**: Click the "Teaching" tab at the top
3. **Add New Skill**: Click the "+" button
4. **Fill in Details**:
   - **Skill Name**: What you can teach (e.g., "JavaScript Programming")
   - **Category**: Choose from Programming, Design, Management, Marketing, Data Science
   - **Proficiency Level**: Rate your expertise (0-100%)
   - **Description**: Explain what you can teach
   - **Experience**: Share your background and experience
   - **Goals**: What you aim to achieve through teaching
   - **Agenda**: List of topics you can cover
5. **Save**: Click "Add Skill" to save

### Adding Learning Skills

1. **Select Learning Tab**: Click the "Learning" tab
2. **Add New Skill**: Click the "+" button
3. **Fill in Details**:
   - **Skill Name**: What you want to learn
   - **Category**: Choose appropriate category
   - **Current Level**: Your current proficiency (0-100%)
   - **Description**: What you want to learn
   - **Goals**: Your learning objectives
   - **Agenda**: Topics you want to cover
4. **Save**: Click "Add Skill" to save

### Managing Skills

#### Editing Skills
1. **Find the Skill**: Locate the skill in your list
2. **Click Edit**: Click the edit icon (pencil)
3. **Update Information**: Modify any fields
4. **Save Changes**: Click "Update Skill"

#### Deleting Skills
1. **Find the Skill**: Locate the skill to delete
2. **Click Delete**: Click the trash icon
3. **Confirm**: Click "Delete" in the confirmation dialog

#### Skill Categories and Colors
- **Programming**: Blue
- **Design**: Purple
- **Management**: Green
- **Marketing**: Orange
- **Data Science**: Teal

## Sessions

Sessions are structured learning meetings where you can teach or learn skills.

### Creating a Session

1. **Navigate to Sessions**: Click "Sessions" in the sidebar
2. **Create New Session**: Click the "+" button
3. **Fill in Session Details**:
   - **Title**: Descriptive name for your session
   - **Date**: When the session will take place
   - **Start Time**: Session start time
   - **End Time**: Session end time
   - **Skill Category**: What skill will be taught
   - **Teaching Mode**: Toggle between teaching and learning
   - **Description**: Detailed description of the session
   - **Max Participants**: How many people can join
   - **Public/Private**: Choose session visibility
   - **Meeting Link**: Add video call link (optional)
   - **Sub Topics**: Specific topics to cover
   - **Focus Keywords**: Key concepts for the session
4. **Save Session**: Click "Create Session"

### Managing Your Sessions

#### Viewing Sessions
- **All Sessions**: See all your sessions
- **Upcoming**: Sessions scheduled for the future
- **Completed**: Past sessions
- **Cancelled**: Cancelled sessions

#### Session Actions
- **Edit**: Modify session details
- **Reschedule**: Change date/time
- **Cancel**: Cancel the session
- **Complete**: Mark as finished
- **View Details**: See full session information

#### Session Status
- **Upcoming**: Scheduled for the future
- **Completed**: Successfully finished
- **Cancelled**: No longer happening

### Joining Sessions

1. **Browse Available Sessions**: Look for public sessions in the community
2. **View Session Details**: Click on a session to see full information
3. **Request to Join**: Click "Request to Join" if required
4. **Get Confirmation**: Wait for host approval
5. **Join Session**: Use the meeting link at the scheduled time

## Exchange Requests

Exchange requests allow you to propose skill swaps with other users.

### Creating an Exchange Request

1. **Find a User**: Browse the "Connect" section or user profiles
2. **View Their Skills**: Check what they can teach and want to learn
3. **Propose Exchange**: Click "Propose Exchange"
4. **Select Skills**:
   - **Offered Skill**: What you can teach them
   - **Requested Skill**: What you want to learn from them
5. **Choose Session**: Select an existing session or create a new one
6. **Add Message**: Write a personalized message explaining the exchange
7. **Send Request**: Click "Send Exchange Request"

### Managing Exchange Requests

#### Received Requests
- **View Requests**: See all requests sent to you
- **Accept**: Agree to the exchange
- **Reject**: Decline the exchange
- **Message**: Communicate with the requester

#### Sent Requests
- **Track Status**: Monitor pending, accepted, or rejected requests
- **Cancel**: Withdraw your request if needed
- **Message**: Follow up with the recipient

#### Request Status
- **Pending**: Waiting for response
- **Accepted**: Exchange approved
- **Rejected**: Exchange declined
- **Cancelled**: Request withdrawn

## Exchange Sessions

Exchange sessions are specialized sessions where two users teach each other different skills.

### Understanding Exchange Sessions

Exchange sessions are unique because:
- **Two-way Learning**: Both participants teach and learn
- **Skill Swap**: Each person offers one skill and learns another
- **Structured Format**: Organized time for both teaching and learning
- **Mutual Benefit**: Both participants gain value

### Managing Exchange Sessions

#### Viewing Your Sessions
1. **Navigate to Exchange Sessions**: Click "Exchange Sessions" in sidebar
2. **Filter Options**: Use filters to find specific sessions
3. **Session Details**: Click on sessions for full information

#### Session Actions
- **Join Meeting**: Access the video call
- **Reschedule**: Change the session time
- **Cancel**: Cancel the session
- **Mark Complete**: End the session
- **Leave Feedback**: Rate the experience

#### Session Preparation
- **Review Materials**: Prepare your teaching content
- **Test Technology**: Ensure your video/audio works
- **Set Agenda**: Plan what you'll cover
- **Prepare Questions**: Have learning objectives ready

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Login Problems
**Issue**: Can't log in to your account
**Solutions**:
- Check your email and password
- Reset your password if needed
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser

#### Session Issues
**Issue**: Can't join or create sessions
**Solutions**:
- Check your internet connection
- Verify session details and timing
- Contact the session host
- Ensure you have the required skills

#### Messaging Problems
**Issue**: Messages not sending or receiving
**Solutions**:
- Refresh the page
- Check your internet connection
- Log out and log back in
- Clear browser cache

#### Skill Management Issues
**Issue**: Can't add or edit skills
**Solutions**:
- Ensure all required fields are filled
- Check skill name for duplicates
- Verify proficiency level is between 0-100
- Try refreshing the page

### Getting Help

#### Support Resources
- **Help Center**: Comprehensive FAQ and guides
- **Community Forum**: Connect with other users
- **Contact Support**: Direct assistance for complex issues

#### Reporting Problems
1. **Document the Issue**: Note what happened and when
2. **Take Screenshots**: Capture error messages or unexpected behavior
3. **Contact Support**: Provide detailed information about the problem
4. **Follow Up**: Check for updates on your support ticket

### Best Practices

#### Preventing Issues
- **Regular Updates**: Keep your browser updated
- **Stable Connection**: Use reliable internet
- **Clear Cache**: Periodically clear browser data
- **Backup Data**: Export important information regularly

#### System Requirements
- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest versions)
- **Internet**: Stable broadband connection
- **Device**: Computer, tablet, or smartphone
- **Storage**: Sufficient space for temporary files
